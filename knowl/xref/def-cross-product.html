Definition 3.4.1. The Cross Product (First Definition).

Given two vectors \(\mathbf u\) and \(\mathbf v\) in \(\mathbb R^3\text{,}\) the cross product \(\mathbf u \times \mathbf v\) is the vector defined by
\begin{equation} \mathbf u \times \mathbf v = \left(\|\mathbf u\|\|\mathbf v\|\sin\theta\right)\hat{n},\tag{3.4.1} \end{equation}
where \(\theta\text{,}\) with \(0 \le \theta \le \pi\text{,}\) is the angle between \(\mathbf u\) and \(\mathbf v\text{,}\) and \(\hat{n}\) is the unit vector orthogonal to both \(\mathbf u\) and \(\mathbf v\) whose direction is given by the right-hand rule.
