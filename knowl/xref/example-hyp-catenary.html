Example 1.6.1. A hanging telephone line.

A telephone line hangs between two poles \(14\) m apart in the shape of the catenary \(y = 20\cosh(x/20) - 15\text{,}\) where \(x\) and \(y\) are measured in meters.
  1. Find the slope of this curve where it meets the right pole.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find the angle \(\theta\) between the line and the pole.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
  1. The function \(y = 20\cosh(x/20) - 15\) is plotted in Figure 1.6.2. To find the slope at the right pole, we differentiate the function with respect to \(x\) and evaluate it at \(x = 7\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} \left.\frac{df}{dx}\right|_{x=7} \amp = \left.\sinh(x/20)\right|_{x=7}\\ \amp = \sinh(7/20) \approx 0.357. \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. We know that \(\tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \theta\right) = \cot(\theta) = 0.357\) and hence \(\theta = \cot^{-1}(0.357) \approx 1.23\) radians.
