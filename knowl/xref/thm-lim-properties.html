Theorem 4.2.3. Properties of Limits.

The following rules hold if \(L\text{,}\) \(M\text{,}\) and \(k\) are real numbers and
\begin{equation*} \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} f(x,y) = L \qquad \text{and} \qquad \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} g(x,y) = M. \end{equation*}
🔗
1. Sum Rule: \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} \bigl(f(x,y)+g(x,y)\bigr) = L + M\)
🔗
2. Difference Rule: \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} \bigl(f(x,y)-g(x,y)\bigr) = L - M\)
🔗
3. Product Rule: \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} \bigl(f(x,y)\cdot g(x,y)\bigr) = L \cdot M\)
🔗
4. Constant Multiple Rule: \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} k f(x,y) = kL\) for any number \(k\)
🔗
5. Quotient Rule: \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} \frac{f(x,y)}{g(x,y)} = \frac{L}{M}\text{,}\) provided \(M \neq 0\)
🔗
6. Power Rule: If \(r\) and \(s\) are integers with no common factors and \(s \neq 0\text{,}\) then \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} \bigl(f(x,y)\bigr)^{r/s} = L^{r/s}\text{,}\) provided \(L^{r/s}\) is a real number. (If \(s\) is even, we assume that \(L \gt 0\text{.}\))
