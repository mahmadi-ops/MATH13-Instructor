Theorem 4.7.12. Theorem II (The Second Derivative Test).

Suppose that \(f(x,y)\) and its first and second partial derivatives are continuous throughout a disk centered at \((a,b)\) and that \(f_x(a,b) = f_y(a,b) = 0\text{.}\) Then
🔗
  1. \(f\) has a local maximum at \((a,b)\) if \(f_{xx} \lt 0\) and \(f_{xx}f_{yy} - f_{xy}^2 \gt 0\) at \((a,b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(f\) has a local minimum at \((a,b)\) if \(f_{xx} \gt 0\) and \(f_{xx}f_{yy} - f_{xy}^2 \gt 0\) at \((a,b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(f\) has a saddle point at \((a,b)\) if \(f_{xx}f_{yy} - f_{xy}^2 \lt 0\) at \((a,b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The test is inconclusive at \((a,b)\) if \(f_{xx}f_{yy} - f_{xy}^2 = 0\) at \((a,b)\text{.}\) In this case, we must find some other way to determine the behavior of \(f\) at \((a,b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
