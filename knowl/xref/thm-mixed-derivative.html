Theorem 4.3.11. The Mixed Derivative Theorem (Clairaut’s Theorem).

If \(f(x,y)\) and its partial derivatives \(f_x\text{,}\) \(f_y\text{,}\) \(f_{xy}\text{,}\) and \(f_{yx}\) are defined throughout an open region containing a point \((a,b)\) and are all continuous at \((a,b)\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation} f_{xy}(a,b) = f_{yx}(a,b).\tag{4.3.5} \end{equation}
