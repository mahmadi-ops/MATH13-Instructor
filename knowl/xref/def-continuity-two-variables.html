Definition 4.2.9. Continuity at a Point and on the Domain.

A function \(f(x,y)\) is continuous at the point \((x_0,y_0)\) if
🔗
1. \(f\) is defined at \((x_0,y_0)\text{,}\)
🔗
2. \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} f(x,y)\) exists,
🔗
3. \(\displaystyle \lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)} f(x,y) = f(x_0,y_0)\text{.}\)
🔗
A function is continuous if it is continuous at every point of its domain.
