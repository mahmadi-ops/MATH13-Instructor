Example 4.3.10. Computing Second Partial Derivatives.

Consider the function \(f(x,y) = \cos(xy^2)\) and evaluate the following partial derivatives.
  1. \(\displaystyle f_{yx}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle f_{xy}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle f_{xx}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Solution.
A.
\begin{align*} f_{yx} \amp = \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\cos(xy^2)\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(-y^2\sin(xy^2)\right)\\ \amp = -2y\sin(xy^2) - 2xy^3\cos(xy^2) \end{align*}
🔗
B.
\begin{align*} f_{xy} \amp = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\cos(xy^2)\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(-2xy\sin(xy^2)\right)\\ \amp = -2y\sin(xy^2) - 2xy^3\cos(xy^2) \end{align*}
🔗
C.
\begin{equation*} f_{xx} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\cos(xy^2)\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(-y^2\sin(xy^2)\right) = -y^4\cos(xy^2) \end{equation*}
