Theorem 3.4.7. Properties of the Cross Product.

If \(\mathbf u\text{,}\) \(\mathbf v\text{,}\) and \(\mathbf w\) are any vectors and \(r\text{,}\) \(s\) are scalars, then
🔗
  1. \(\displaystyle (r\mathbf u) \times (s\mathbf v) = (rs)(\mathbf u \times \mathbf v)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \mathbf u \times (\mathbf v + \mathbf w) = \mathbf u \times \mathbf v + \mathbf u \times \mathbf w\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle (\mathbf v + \mathbf w) \times \mathbf u = \mathbf v \times \mathbf u + \mathbf w \times \mathbf u\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\displaystyle \mathbf v \times \mathbf u = -(\mathbf u \times \mathbf v)\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(\displaystyle \mathbf 0 \times \mathbf u = \mathbf 0\)
    🔗
    🔗
