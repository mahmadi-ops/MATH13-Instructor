Instructions.
The surface shown is the level surface \(g(x,y,z) = x^2+y^2-z^2 = c\text{.}\) Drag the \(c\) slider, or press “Play” to sweep \(c\) from \(-4\) to \(4\) and back. Every horizontal slice is a circle of radius \(\sqrt{c+z^2}\text{:}\) for \(c \gt 0\) that radius is defined for every \(z\text{,}\) so the surface is a single sheet pinched to a waist of radius \(\sqrt c\) in the \(xy\)-plane; for \(c \lt 0\) it needs \(|z| \ge \sqrt{-c}\text{,}\) so the surface breaks into two bowls with a gap between them; and at \(c = 0\) the waist and the gap both shrink to the single point where the two nappes of the cone meet. The buttons jump straight to the three cases. “Cone ghost” keeps the \(c = 0\) cone on screen faintly, so you can watch both hyperboloids hug it as \(|c|\) gets small. Drag the figure to change the viewpoint, or press “Spin” to let it turn.
